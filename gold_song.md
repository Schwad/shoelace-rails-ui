# GOLD — a Connor Price-flavored Suno banger

**Subject:** ZAR's new product "GOLD" — buy gold for as little as a dollar in the app.

---

## Suno Style Prompt

```
Bouncy melodic hip-hop, Connor Price style, bright pop-rap, sunny afternoon
energy, snappy boom-bap drums with a trap hi-hat shuffle, plucky guitar loop,
warm 70s funk bass, finger snaps, hand claps, vinyl crackle, smooth featured
R&B vocalist on the hook, confident charming flow, witty wordplay, big group
"hey!" chants, whistled outro. Tempo ~96 BPM. Key: A minor. Clean, radio-ready,
feel-good summer single. No swearing.
```

## Production Notes for Suno

- **Vibe:** Connor Price meets Anderson .Paak meets a sunlit Sunday drive.
- **Hook delivery:** Featured singer should sound playful, slightly raspy, ad-libbing "woah-oh"s.
- **Drums:** Crisp snare on the 2 and 4, hi-hat triplets in the pre-chorus, drop to claps in the bridge.
- **Instruments:** Clean electric guitar loop (4-bar), upright-ish funk bass, occasional brass stab on "GOLD."
- **Mix:** Warm, vinyl-tinged, master loud but not crushed. Whistled outro mixed wide.
- **Length target:** 2:45–3:10. Radio edit friendly.
